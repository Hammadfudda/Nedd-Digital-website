Our Services

Detailed QuickBooks and Power BI services designed for small & medium businesses, accountants and financial managers.

💼

📌 QuickBooks Setup & Training

We configure QuickBooks for your business: chart of accounts setup, tax settings, invoicing, payment integration and secure user roles. Includes step-by-step training for your staff so they can manage daily operations confidently.

🧾

🧹 QuickBooks Cleanup & Bookkeeping

Fix account inconsistencies, reconcile bank accounts, clean historical data and correct invoices/payments. We deliver a clean, audit-ready books state so month-end closes faster and forecasting is more reliable.

📊

📈 Power BI Dashboard Creation

Custom Power BI dashboards for cash flow, profit by product, AR aging, expense trends and executive summaries. Role-based views and printable reports designed for managers and accountants.

🔁

🔄 Power BI Data Automation

Automate ETL and scheduled refreshes from QuickBooks and other sources. We build dataflows that keep dashboards fresh, reduce manual reporting and integrate with Excel/CSV exports when needed.
