❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the FREE historical dashboard with bookkeeping packages? ▼

With every bookkeeping package, you get 1 custom Power BI dashboard built using the last 3 years of historical data from your QuickBooks. This is a one-time build showing past trends and KPIs. It does NOT include automated refresh, real-time updates, or ongoing maintenance. For live dashboards with automated refresh, see our standalone Dashboard Packages above.

Why do Dashboard Packages require a 12-month contract? ▼

Building a custom dashboard requires significant upfront investment in design, data modeling, integration, and testing. The 12-month commitment ensures ongoing quality, support, and automated refresh services. After 12 months, you can continue month-to-month or take ownership of the dashboard files and manage them yourself.

What happens if I cancel my Dashboard contract early? ▼

Early cancellation requires a $1,000 termination fee plus 50% of the remaining monthly payments. For example, if you cancel at month 6 on the Starter plan ($400/month), you'd pay: $1,000 fee + ($400 × 50% × 6 months) = $2,200 total. This protects our investment in building your custom solution.

Do I need to purchase Power BI licenses? ▼

Yes, Power BI requires licenses for each user viewing dashboards. Power BI Pro costs $10/user/month directly from Microsoft (or Power BI Premium for organization-wide access). We'll help you determine the best licensing option and set this up during onboarding.

What's the difference between "monthly transaction entry" and "weekly" or "daily"? ▼

Monthly entry (Essential): Transactions are recorded once per month at month-end (28-30th). Weekly entry (Professional): Transactions updated every week for more current data. Daily processing (Enterprise): Transactions entered daily for real-time accuracy. Choose based on how current you need your books to be.

What's the difference between "Basic AR/AP Tracking" and "Full AR/AP Management"? ▼

Basic Tracking (Essential): We record invoices and bills in QuickBooks and track who owes what. No follow-ups or reminders. Full Management (Professional/Enterprise): We actively manage the process including sending payment reminders, following up on overdue accounts, scheduling vendor payments, and providing cash flow insights.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my package? ▼

Absolutely! You can upgrade anytime. For downgrades, we require 30 days notice. We'll work with you to find the right fit as your business grows or changes.

What if my QuickBooks is a mess? ▼

No problem! We offer QuickBooks cleanup services ($1,000-$2,500). We'll audit your file, fix errors, reconcile accounts, and get everything organized before starting ongoing bookkeeping.

How long does setup take? ▼

Bookkeeping setup: 1-2 weeks. Historical dashboard (included with bookkeeping): 2-3 weeks. Live dashboard with automation (standalone packages): 2-4 weeks. We'll have you up and running within 4-6 weeks total for most projects.

What if I already have a bookkeeper? ▼

Perfect! You can purchase our standalone Power BI dashboard packages. We'll work with your existing bookkeeper to ensure data quality and build dashboards on top of your clean QuickBooks data. Note: These require a 12-month commitment and include ongoing automated refresh.

Can I get just a dashboard without a 12-month contract? ▼

Yes! Our One-Time Dashboard Build option ($2,500-$5,000) is perfect for clients who need a custom dashboard without ongoing service. You'll get a fully functional dashboard with historical data, training, and complete ownership. However, this does NOT include automated refresh or ongoing support. You'll manage the dashboard yourself, and future updates are available at $150/hour if needed.

Do you work with industries other than those listed? ▼

Yes! We've worked with construction, e-commerce, professional services, healthcare, restaurants, manufacturing, and more. Every dashboard is custom-built for your specific industry and KPIs.
