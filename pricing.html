❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the FREE dashboard? ▼

With every bookkeeping package, you get 1 custom Power BI dashboard with up to 5 key metrics of your choice (e.g., cash flow, P&L summary, AR aging, revenue trends, expense breakdown). It includes daily data refresh and mobile access.

Do I need to purchase Power BI licenses? ▼

Yes, Power BI requires licenses for each user viewing dashboards. Power BI Pro costs $10/user/month directly from Microsoft. We'll help you set this up during onboarding.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my package? ▼

Absolutely! You can upgrade anytime. For downgrades, we require 30 days notice. We'll work with you to find the right fit as your business grows.

What if my QuickBooks is a mess? ▼

No problem! We offer QuickBooks cleanup services ($1,000-$2,500). We'll audit your file, fix errors, reconcile accounts, and get everything organized before starting ongoing bookkeeping.

How long does setup take? ▼

Bookkeeping setup: 1-2 weeks. Dashboard development: 2-4 weeks depending on complexity. We'll have you up and running within 4-6 weeks total for most projects.

What if I already have a bookkeeper? ▼

Perfect! You can just purchase our standalone Power BI dashboard packages. We'll work with your existing bookkeeper to ensure data quality and build dashboards on top of your clean QuickBooks data.

Do you work with industries other than those listed? ▼

Yes! We've worked with construction, e-commerce, professional services, healthcare, restaurants, and more. Every dashboard is custom-built for your specific industry and KPIs.
